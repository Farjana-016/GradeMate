          4. About GradeMate

          v1.1.0

          A modern, private, and precise academic management platform.

          GradeMate is an academic calculator and grade management web application built for college and university students. All calculation engines execute locally in your web browser with zero remote telemetry, tracking, or cloud dependencies.

          Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA)

          The weighted average of grade points earned across all registered subjects within a single semester:

          SGPA = ∑(Course Credits × Grade Points) ÷ ∑Course Credits

          Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA)

          The credit-weighted cumulative average of all completed semesters across degree progression:

          CGPA = ∑(Semester SGPA × Semester Credits) ÷ ∑Semester Credits
